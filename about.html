About Khothe Tourism

Lesotho is a high-altitude, landlocked kingdom encircled completely by South Africa. Known proudly as the "Kingdom in the Sky," it is the only independent state on Earth that lies entirely above 1,400 meters in elevation.

At Lesotho Tourism, our mission is to showcase the breathtaking natural splendor, rich Basotho heritage, alpine climates, and pristine river networks to travelers from all around the world.

Whether you are traversing rugged mountain passes on horseback, chasing roaring cascades like Maletsunyane Falls, or seeking winter thrills at Afriski Mountain Resort, Lesotho Tourism delivers unforgettable and authentic journeys.
